				<p>My name is Kyle Dawson. I'm a Lead Data Scientist in Geospatial Intelligence at <a href="https://www.centerpointenergy.com/">CenterPoint Energy</a> in Houston, TX, based out of CenterPoint Energy Tower. In my role, I build machine learning and geospatial models that turn large, messy data into decisions for a safer, more resilient, and more sustainable energy system. Before that, I received my PhD in Atmospheric Science from <a href="https://meas.sciences.ncsu.edu/">North Carolina State University</a> in Raleigh, NC. I'm passionate about our environment and take seriously our responsibility for maintaining our <i>generally</i> comfortable surroundings, and I love working at the intersection of information-as-data and environmental stewardship.</p>
