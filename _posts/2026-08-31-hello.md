I'm excited to share that I've joined **CenterPoint Energy** in Houston, TX as a
**Lead Data Scientist in Geospatial Intelligence**. It's a natural next step in a
career spent at the intersection of data science and environmental stewardship — from
aerosol research at NASA Langley, to methane and greenhouse-gas quantification at
SeekOps, and now to building geospatial and machine learning capabilities for a more
reliable and sustainable energy system.

I'll use this space to share occasional notes on the work I care about: geospatial
intelligence, applied machine learning, remote sensing, and the messy, rewarding
process of turning real-world data into decisions.

More to come. In the meantime, take a look at my [resume](/resume.html),
[publications](/publications.html), and [projects](/projects.html) — or
[get in touch](/landing.html).
